01

Introduction

NoTrackRun ("we", "our", "the app") is a privacy-first running application developed by dev.henin.notrackrun. We believe that connected sport should never come at the cost of your personal freedom.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle — and deliberately don't handle — your data. Our core principle: collect nothing that isn't strictly necessary, and give you full control over everything that is.

Zero profiling No ads GDPR compliant EU hosted No mandatory accounts
02

Data We Collect

NoTrackRun is designed to operate with no personal data by default. No name, no email, no account — nothing that identifies you is required to use the app.

Classic Mode
Records performance metrics (time, distance, pace, elevation) and GPS route — stored locally on your device only. Never uploaded to our servers without explicit action from you.

"No Track" Mode
Records only activity metrics. GPS location tracking is fully disabled. No route is stored — not locally, not remotely. You can run completely invisibly.

Beta Sign-up (optional)
If you register for our beta, we collect your email address for a single notification. It is deleted immediately after sending.
03

Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be explicit about what we refuse to collect — regardless of industry norms:

No identity data No device fingerprint No location history No behavioral tracking No ad identifiers No third-party analytics No cloud sync without consent No social login
04

Coaches & Organizations

For clubs, coaches, and companies using our web interface: access is managed through anonymous access codes. Athletes never share personal data with the platform — only their performance results, which are tied to a code, never a person.

Organizations access a private management space. No athlete identification is required or possible at the platform level. All data processed through the web interface is stored on European servers.

Code-based architecture: Athletes → anonymous code → workouts & sync. No name, no email, no profile.
05

Data Storage & Security

All performance data that leaves your device is stored exclusively on servers located within the European Union, in accordance with GDPR requirements and the principle of European digital sovereignty.

Local data (GPS traces, session records) resides only on your device and can be deleted at any time — including retroactively after a session in "No Track" mode.

EU servers only Local-first architecture On-device deletion Encrypted in transit
06

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding any data we may hold:

Right of access — Know what data we hold on you.
Right to erasure — Request complete deletion at any time.
Right to portability — Export your data in a standard format.
Right to object — Opt out of any processing at any time.
Right to rectification — Correct any inaccurate information.

Since we collect almost nothing, exercising these rights is straightforward. Contact us at any time.
07

Third Parties & Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party — advertising platforms, data brokers, or analytics services.

Social sharing (if you choose to share a run result) is entirely optional and initiated only by you. We design sharing features to expose performance metrics only — never your location or route unless you explicitly include it.
